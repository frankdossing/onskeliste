# Glossary

Domain terms for WishiCloud. Terms are in Danish, as used on the site.

**Ønskeliste** (wishlist)
A named collection of wishes that one person sends to family til en lejlighed (jul, fødselsdag). Shown as the whole page.

**Ønske** (wish)
One thing the wishlist owner would like to receive. Has a name, optional description, and one or more butik options.

**Kategori** (category)
A group of wishes on the list, e.g. "Mad- & drikkeoplevelser" or "Diverse".

**Butik** (shop)
A retailer where a wish can be bought, e.g. Magasin, Vinhuset, Whisky.dk, Bog & Idé.

**Butiksvalg** (shop option)
One concrete product listing at one shop for a wish. A wish can have several shop options, possibly at the same shop. Each option has a link, and optionally a price.

**Se i butik** (see in shop)
The action that opens the shop options for a wish in new tabs.

**Reservation** (reservation)
A visitor's claim that they will buy a wish, so nobody else buys it too. *Currently fake: nothing is saved, and the reaction is a visual surprise only.*
